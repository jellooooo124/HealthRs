Recipes: 522,517 raw -> 514,653 after cleaning (removed: 3,111 with 0 kcal, 2,615 > 5000 kcal/serving, 427 sodium > 30,664 mg (99.9th pct), 1,711 without ingredients) -> 45,926 in the k-core catalog.
Ratings of 0 (76,248, reviews without stars) are dropped; ratings 1–3 on catalog recipes (26,873 pairs) are excluded from negative sampling.

## 5. Sanity check: our labels vs Food.com keyword tags

| Food.com tag | Condition | Tagged recipes | Compliant (tagged) | Compliant (untagged) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Very Low Carbs | Diabetes | 1,891 | 99.6% | 77.0% |
| Healthy | Diabetes | 8,347 | 67.0% | 80.3% |
| Healthy | Hypertension | 8,347 | 65.4% | 38.5% |
| Low Cholesterol | Hypertension | 7,979 | 67.8% | 38.2% |
| Dessert | Diabetes | 3,364 | 59.7% | 79.3% |

Tags are assigned by Food.com independently of our thresholds. 'Very Low Carbs', 'Low Cholesterol' and 'Dessert' agree with our labels in the expected direction. 'Healthy' is *less* diabetes-compliant than untagged recipes: Food.com's 'Healthy' mostly means low-fat (often fruit-, grain- or sugar-heavy), which is exactly why generic 'healthy' tags are not enough for condition-specific recommendation. No tag exists for sodium or purines.
