# Offline evaluation (test split)

Generated by `scripts/run_offline.py`. Temporal leave-one-out on 19,455 warm users; full ranking over 45,926 recipes (train/val positives masked). Trained models: mean ± std over seeds [2026, 2027, 2028]. Selected configs (val NDCG@10, coverage@10 ≥ 1%): BPR-MF: dim 32, L2 0.01; A: dim 32, L2 0.01. Recall@10 = HR@10 for a single held-out item. violation@10: share of top-10 recipes violating ≥1 of the user's conditions (users with ≥1 condition); user_violation@10: share of those users with ≥1 violating recipe; allergen@10: share of top-10 recipes containing one of the user's allergens (users with ≥1 allergy).
